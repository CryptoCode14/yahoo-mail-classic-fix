New in v3.1 — the Inbox icon keeps count, and Unread moves up

The left rail is a column of icons (Inbox, Sent, Drafts…). Now the Inbox icon carries a purple count badge — Yahoo's own unread total, the same number as the browser tab title, never a count of just the rows on screen. One digit is a circle, two a neat pill, anything over 99 shows as 99+, and at zero the badge disappears completely. Under it sits the other half of the change: Yahoo buries the Unread view in the “•••” menu — the fix moves it into the rail directly under Inbox, with no number on it, because the count already lives on Inbox.
